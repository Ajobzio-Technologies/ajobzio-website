Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Ajobzio collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit or use ajobzio.com.

Effective Date: 25 August 2026 Last Updated: 25 August 2026

Ajobzio Technologies Private Limited (“Ajobzio,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates https://ajobzio.com (the “Site”). This policy applies to the public website, including the enquire form. When our job marketplace launches additional features, we will update this policy if we collect new categories of information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you provide it, as well as limited technical information when you visit the Site.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact forms & enquiries: When you contact us through the enquire form or email, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Email correspondence: If you write to us at admin@ajobzio.com or support@ajobzio.com, we keep the details you send so we can respond and follow up.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical & log data: Our hosting provider may automatically collect standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and pages visited, for security and reliability.
  • Fonts: The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address and related technical data when those fonts are requested. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not currently use Google Analytics or similar advertising pixels on the Site. We do not collect payment card numbers on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries, support requests, or contact form submissions.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or illegal activity.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Site and conduct business. They receive access only as needed to perform their tasks on our behalf.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Site uses limited cookies and similar technologies as needed for basic operation and security. We do not currently place analytics or advertising cookies.

You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the Site work.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, respond to enquiries, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Enquiry messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable follow-up and record-keeping.

6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we rectify inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: Request that we erase your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Opt-out: Ask us to stop using your information for further contact, other than as required to complete an existing request.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
